# SII-HRRP

Second-order Shapley interaction analysis for HRRP scattering-center cooperation and reduced-set construction.

## Method

Physically extracted scattering centers are treated as players, and the second-order Shapley Interaction Index (SII) is used to quantify pairwise synergy and antagonism among them.

## Key Findings

- The interaction prior does not guarantee a minimal reduced set.
- At matched cardinality, interaction-aware selection preserves more of the cooperative structure.
- The effect depends on the backbone and the data.
- In simulated ECM, deception and stealth outcomes are mixed, with both positive and negative results.
